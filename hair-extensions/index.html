Extensions FAQ

What Extensions Do We Use?

We offer luxury hair extensions that blend seamlessly and last 12 to 20 weeks with proper care. Your extensions will be custom-matched to your natural color and installed by a certified stylist using either keratin bond (k-tip) or silk weft methods — two of the most natural-looking, comfortable options available.

Pricing starts at $250 and varies depending on your goals and hair type. Before installation, we always begin with a one-on-one consultation to build your perfect plan.

For k-tip clients, new hair is used with each cycle to maintain integrity and cleanliness. Removal is quick and painless, typically taking around 30 minutes.

How to Care for Your Extensions

After installation, you’ll need to wait 24 hours before washing your hair — and a little longer before tying it up, especially with silk wefts. Once you’re in the swing of it, extensions are easy to manage: style, blow dry, or air dry just like your natural hair.

A few important care tips:
— Use a heat protectant before styling
— Avoid wet buns and don’t brush near the bonds
— Skip silicones and sunscreen on your hair to prevent color changes
— We recommend Oway’s Glossy Nectar or Reverie’s Ever Oil to keep hair soft and radiant

Shedding a few extensions over time is totally normal — especially if worn past the recommended timeframe.
