Hair System FAQ

What is a “hair system”?

Hair systems are non-surgical, customized hair replacement solutions that seamlessly blend with your natural hair. Gone are the days of grandpa’s toupee. Restore confidence and feel more like yourself with a personalized hair system. This is not an accessory — it’s a transformation.

How do I begin my transformation journey?

The journey began the moment your curiosity began. Your next step is to schedule a consultation by emailing tyler@freehairsalon.com. During our consultation we will consider all options in order to come up with a plan to provide the best hair system solution. Our goal is to find the ideal product and look to meet your expectations.

What are hair systems made from?

Not all systems are created equal. My preference is to only use the highest quality products available. Durable, invisible bases and 100% real human hair. Hair system bases are made from lace, polyurethane or a hybrid of the two. I’ve partnered with the industry-leading Hairskeen to provide what I believe are the best systems on the market.

What is the life cycle of my hair system?

In short, your hair system will perform and last longer with better care. Our first installation is when the system will be fully personalized to your scalp and haircut. Every following maintenance appointment comes with a haircut and a reinstallation of the hair system. Those time frames look different for every person depending on a few variables we will discuss during consultation.

What is the at-home care for my hair system like?

The system is real hair so you can treat it as such! Shampoo it. Style it. Exercise in it. Part of our consultation will help us determine what changes will bring the most out of your hair style. Wearing a system is a huge transformation so adjustments will be necessary. Finding the right product and care routine makes all the difference. Together we will create a plan to maximize the lifespan of your hair system.

How much does it cost?

Consultations require a $50 deposit which will contribute to the cost of a new system. New hair system installation starts at $650 and maintenance appointments start at $120.
